# Terms of Service - Draft

Operational draft for Enlace Ganadero.

This document is an initial operational draft and must be reviewed by legal counsel before production use.

## Purpose

These terms describe the expected use of Enlace Ganadero as a digital platform for cattle-related catalog, listings, memberships, promotion, advertising, sponsorships, auctions as a complementary module, and administrative services.

## Platform Role

Enlace Ganadero provides digital tools and platform services. Enlace Ganadero does not sell cattle, does not buy cattle, does not own animals listed by users, and does not act as the buyer or seller in cattle transactions between users.

Enlace Ganadero does not invoice the purchase or sale value of cattle. Any tax, legal, operational, or documentary obligation related to a cattle sale remains the responsibility of the buyer and seller.

## User Accounts

Users are responsible for:

- Providing accurate account information.
- Protecting access credentials.
- Using the platform in good faith.
- Keeping contact, fiscal, and operational information current.
- Complying with applicable laws and regulations.

Enlace Ganadero may restrict, suspend, or terminate access when an account creates operational, legal, security, payment, or abuse risk.

## Catalog And Listings

Users may create catalog records and listings for animals they own or are authorized to represent. Users are responsible for the accuracy of animal information, media, documents, registry data, price, location, and availability.

Enlace Ganadero may remove or restrict listings that appear fraudulent, misleading, duplicated, illegal, unsafe, or contrary to platform policies.

## Memberships

Memberships provide access to platform benefits such as listing capacity, tools, visibility, and other digital services. Paid memberships may require checkout through an authorized payment processor.

Benefits, limits, prices, billing periods, and availability may vary by plan and may be updated according to the applicable membership policy.

## Publications And Promotion

Premium listings, featured listings, sponsorships, advertisements, and similar services are platform visibility or promotional services. They do not imply that Enlace Ganadero guarantees a sale, acts as seller, or assumes tax responsibility for cattle transactions.

## Auctions

Auctions are a complementary module. They facilitate a digital process between users, but Enlace Ganadero is not the owner, buyer, or seller of auctioned animals.

Any commercial agreement, delivery, payment between parties, health documentation, transport, tax compliance, and legal responsibility remain with the participating users.

## Restrictions

Users must not:

- Publish animals they do not own or are not authorized to represent.
- Provide false or misleading information.
- Attempt unauthorized access.
- Abuse payment, membership, or support processes.
- Upload unsafe, illegal, or infringing content.
- Use the platform for fraud, money laundering, or illegal activity.
- Circumvent membership limits or enforcement.

## Payments

Payments made through Enlace Ganadero correspond to platform services, not cattle purchases. Payment processing may be handled by external providers such as Stripe.

Enlace Ganadero does not store card PAN, CVC, or full card credentials.

## Limitation Of Responsibility

Enlace Ganadero is not responsible for user-to-user negotiations, cattle condition, delivery, transportation, ownership disputes, health certificates, registry accuracy, or tax obligations between buyer and seller.

Platform availability may depend on third-party services including hosting, database, payment processors, and communication providers.

## Suspension Of Account

An account may be suspended when there are payment failures, policy violations, suspected fraud, security incidents, abuse, or unresolved administrative issues.

Suspension may block new publications or access to certain benefits, but existing listings are not automatically deleted unless a separate policy or legal requirement applies.

## Changes

These terms may be updated before production launch. Final terms must be approved by legal counsel before public use.
