# Membership Policy - Draft

Operational draft for Enlace Ganadero.

This document is an initial operational draft and must be reviewed by legal counsel before production use.

## Purpose

This policy defines expected membership behavior for Enlace Ganadero.

## Free Membership

Every user may receive a Free membership automatically. Free membership provides baseline access and may include limited publication capacity or other basic benefits.

Legacy users without membership should receive Free membership when their membership state is evaluated.

## Paid Plans

Paid plans may provide additional benefits such as higher active listing capacity, visibility options, platform tools, or other digital services.

Paid plan benefits are services of Enlace Ganadero and do not cover cattle sale taxes, cattle ownership, or buyer-seller obligations.

## Listing Limits

Membership plans may define active listing limits. The platform may block new published listings when the user has reached plan capacity.

Existing listings should not be deleted or hidden only because a user downgrades, cancels, or enters suspended status.

## Upgrades

Changing from Free to a paid plan, or from one paid plan to a higher paid plan, may require checkout.

The platform must not activate a paid upgrade until the required payment confirmation is processed through the approved payment flow.

## Downgrades

Downgrades may be scheduled for the end of the current period. The user keeps current plan benefits until the period ends.

When a downgrade is applied, new publications may be limited by the lower plan, but existing listings are not automatically removed.

## Cancellation

Cancellation normally uses cancelAtPeriodEnd. The user keeps benefits until the current period ends, unless administrative action or policy violation requires otherwise.

After the period ends, the paid membership may be cancelled and Free membership should be active.

## Suspension

A membership may enter grace_period, in_dunning, or suspended status due to payment issues or administrative action.

Suspended memberships may block new publications while preserving existing listings.

## Reactivation

If cancellation has not yet taken effect, a user may reactivate and keep the current plan. Reactivation clears pending cancellation fields where applicable.

If a membership has been cancelled or suspended, administrative review may be required depending on the case.

## Administrative Review

Admins may review membership state, usage, change logs, dunning state, payment records, notifications, and audit trails to resolve support cases.

## Changes

This policy may be updated before production launch. Final policy must be approved by legal counsel before public use.
