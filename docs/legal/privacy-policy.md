# Privacy Policy - Draft

Operational draft for Enlace Ganadero.

This document is an initial operational draft and must be reviewed by legal counsel before production use.

## Purpose

This policy describes how Enlace Ganadero expects to handle account, contact, platform, payment, and fiscal data.

## Account Data

The platform may process:

- Name.
- Email.
- Role.
- Account status.
- Authentication metadata.
- Membership state.
- Catalog and listing activity.

Users are responsible for keeping account information accurate.

## Contact Data

Contact data may be used for account operation, membership support, payment support, administrative follow-up, and user-requested services.

## Fiscal Data

When fiscal features are used, Enlace Ganadero may process fiscal profile data such as RFC, legal name, fiscal regime, postal code, CFDI use, and billing email.

Fiscal data is used for platform services and future invoicing workflows. Enlace Ganadero does not invoice cattle sales between users.

## Payment Data

Payments may be processed by external providers such as Stripe. Enlace Ganadero should store operational payment references, checkout references, customer references, payment status, webhook identifiers, and audit records.

Enlace Ganadero must not store card PAN, CVC, full card number, or sensitive card credentials.

## External Providers

The platform may integrate with external providers including:

- Stripe for payment processing.
- Facturama or another PAC for future fiscal provider workflows.
- Render for hosting.
- MongoDB Atlas for database services.

External providers may process data according to their own terms and privacy policies.

## Technical Cookies

The platform may use technical cookies for authentication, refresh tokens, CSRF protection, and session security. These cookies are necessary for safe platform operation.

## Security

Enlace Ganadero uses application-level controls including authentication, authorization, CSRF protection, role checks, audit trails, and secret handling practices.

No security system is absolute. Users should report suspicious activity promptly.

## User Rights

Users may request review, correction, or deletion of applicable personal data, subject to legal, fiscal, security, fraud-prevention, and operational retention requirements.

## Data Retention

Operational records such as audit logs, payment records, webhook logs, membership changes, and fiscal preparation records may be retained to reconstruct incidents, comply with obligations, and support users.

## Changes

This policy may be updated before production launch. Final policy must be approved by legal counsel before public use.
